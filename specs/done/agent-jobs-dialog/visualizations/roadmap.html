Integrations out, jobs into a door

An agent's card stops answering for what the drive is plugged into, and stops housing a panel of its work. Jobs becomes a door beside Settings that opens every job the agent has — with the selected job's session live beside the list. A job is a session, which is why the dialog can show the real conversation rather than a drawing of one.
