# Services

Duet exposes paid third-party APIs through a catalog. Search with
`duet services search`, inspect a service with `duet services show`, read the
linked vendor documentation, then call an allowed route with `duet services call`.
Duet injects vendor authentication; callers provide their Duet key and select a
workspace with `x-duet-workspace` when needed.

Successful calls are billed at vendor cost plus Duet's standard margin and
return the customer charge in `x-duet-charge-usd`. Errors are not charged.
Routes outside the catalog return 404. Shared resources are readable and usable
by design, while mutation belongs to the creating workspace and temporary items
follow the pool TTL.
