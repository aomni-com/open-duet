# Positioning

**The best AI for writing, images, video and voice, plus agents that browse
the web, work in your apps, research people and companies, run on their own,
and publish what they build. One subscription. Nothing to set up.**

Short form, where space is tight: **Everything state-of-the-art AI can do, in
one subscription.** Use it only next to concrete examples. On its own, "state
of the art" means nothing to someone who doesn't know what that looks like.

Duet is one subscription that keeps you at the frontier of AI without you
having to keep up with it. Every service and feature a state-of-the-art agent
needs is built in and already wired together: the best models, a computer,
a browser, the tools you work in, data and media services, file sharing, and
app hosting. You talk to your agents in plain language. Duet does the
fidgeting.

## Who it's for

**The AI-curious who want the best and will pay for it, but don't want to
become engineers to get it.** Founders, operators, consultants, marketers,
small-business owners: people who read about what AI agents can do now and
want that for themselves, today.

What stops them is not money. It's the setup. The state of the art today is
a stack: a coding agent in a terminal, model keys from three providers, a
scraping service, a browser automation tool, a place to run things around
the clock, somewhere to host what gets built, and constant retuning as new
models ship every month. Tools like Claude Code and Codex are powerful, but
they are built for programmers, and this audience doesn't want to touch them.

Duet is that whole stack, already assembled, behind a chat.

Not for: developers who enjoy assembling their own stack (they can still
plug in; see below), or people who want only a free chatbot.

## What one subscription gets you

Each item is something the user would otherwise have to find, sign up for,
configure, connect, pay for separately, and keep current.

- **The best model for every task.** The leading models for text, images,
  video and voice. You never pick a model. Duet routes each task to the
  strongest one and moves to new models as they ship.
- **A computer that never sleeps.** Every workspace runs on its own always-on
  machine, so agents keep working when your laptop is closed.
- **A browser of its own.** Agents click, type, log in and get things done on
  the real web, and you can watch or take over at any time.
- **Your tools, connected.** Gmail, Slack, Notion, Google Drive and thousands
  more, connected with a click.
- **Research and data services built in.** Web research, company and people
  data, contact details, SEO and social data, voice. No separate accounts.
- **Agents that remember and improve.** Each agent keeps its instructions,
  skills and memory, so it gets better at your work over time, and all of it
  is yours.
- **Runs on its own.** Agents work on a schedule or when something happens,
  so the morning report is done before you sit down.
- **Files and apps.** One shared Drive for your agents and your people. When
  an agent builds a dashboard, tool or site, it goes live at a real link.

Today: all supported. Names, counts and sizes come from the product's own
config (`@repo/contract`, the service catalog); never hand-write them in copy.

## Share it with anyone

Anything in the workspace can go to someone else, and they need no setup of
their own. Selling is sharing with someone who pays.

- **Agents.** Invite people into a workspace and they talk to its agents.
  Viewers can use every agent but can't change how it works. Give a client a
  workspace of their own with your agent inside: their data stays theirs, and
  improving the agent once reaches every client's copy.
- **Files.** Share a folder by email, or publish it at a public link anyone
  can view or download.
- **Apps.** Share what an agent built with chosen people, or put it on its own
  domain for everyone.
- **To software.** Duet's open API lets other products start work with an
  agent and read the result.

Today: all shipped. Gap: there is no paid marketplace, so don't promise
discovery or payouts.

## For people who already use an agent

A secondary path, not the pitch. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, ChatGPT or any
MCP client can connect through the Duet CLI or MCP and use Duet's models,
services, files and memory on the same subscription, and even hand work to
the agents in the workspace. Mention it where developers are the audience
(docs, the README, `/l/` pages aimed at them); keep it off the main pitch.

## What it is, and isn't

- **Is it a chatbot?** No. A chatbot answers. Duet's agents do the work: they
  use a browser, run on a schedule, use your tools, and build things.
- **Is it like Claude Code or Codex?** Those are excellent tools for
  programmers, run from a terminal. Duet gives you the same caliber of agent
  without the terminal, the setup or the code.
- **Is it like a ChatGPT or Claude subscription?** Those give you one company's
  models in a chat. Duet gives you every leading model plus everything an agent
  needs to act, in one subscription.
- **Is it like Zapier?** No flows to draw. You say what you want in plain
  language and the agent works out the steps.

## Pricing facts

One subscription per workspace, with monthly credits that cover the models and
services its agents use. No per-seat charge, and no separate bills from
model or data vendors. Start free with a 7-day trial. Plans live on the
pricing page, which reads them from the product. Model rates by name are never
public; customers see quality levels. Duet does not train on customer files.

## What this means for messaging

- **Lead with "state of the art, no setup", and show what that means.** The
  promise is being at the frontier without keeping up with it: one
  subscription, everything built in, always current. Never assume the reader
  knows what the frontier includes. Most think AI means a text chatbot, so
  name the rest every time: images, video, voice, an agent using a browser,
  working in their apps, researching people and companies, running on a
  schedule, shipping a live site.
- **Speak plainly.** Main copy never needs MCP, API, CLI, VM, terminal, or
  model names to make its point. Say what the agent does, not how it's wired.
  Technical terms belong on developer pages.
- **Sell the outcome, then the inventory.** "Your agent researched 40
  prospects and drafted the emails overnight" before "built-in data services".
  The feature list proves the promise; it isn't the promise.
- **Make the money a relief, not a hurdle.** The audience has budget. What it
  lacks is time and technical depth. One bill replaces a dozen accounts.
- **Keep ownership visible.** The agents, their memory and their work are
  yours, as plain files.
- **Back every claim with the product.** A capability with only an interface
  behind it stays out of copy until it ships.
- One indexed lander, with other pitches on noindexed `/l/<slug>` pages
  ([lineup](specs/lander-refresh/LINEUP.md)). The homepage sells this frame;
  consultants are one use case of it, on an `/l/` lander.
