# Welcome to your Duet workspace

This workspace is shared by you and your agents. Files created here stay available
across sessions and can be organized, previewed, and published from Duet.

Start with the sample document, deck, and short clip in this folder. The
[`hello-app`](hello-app/) folder is a sample app that came with the workspace —
open the **hello** app in Duet to see it running.

Everything here is an example. Change it, or delete it; nothing depends on it.
When you want an app of your own, just ask Duet in chat — *"build me a dashboard
for our weekly numbers"* — and it builds the app, runs it, and hosts it here.
