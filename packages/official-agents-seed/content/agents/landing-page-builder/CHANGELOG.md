# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial release.

- Builds a product's marketing surface from scratch — main landing page, pages for ads, pages meant to be found — or improves and audits what exists.
- Names every page's kind before building it; the kind decides indexing, linking, and chrome, and the halfway states are refused.
- One primary action per page, exactly one ask on pages built for ads.
- Message match runs end to end: the source's sentence, the headline, and what the visitor sees after acting are one promise.
- Refuses to launch a page that cannot be measured, and never invents a business fact.
- Bundles the marketing-pages rulebook — page classes, indexing and linking rules, conversion and SEO practice, audit sweeps.
