# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial release.

- The opportunity gate: NEW / REFRESH / FOLD IN / REJECT, backed by a distinct-job test against the pages that already exist, with "related but different" explicitly not a pass.
- A nine-stage artifact chain under `seo/<slug>/`, one file per stage, mirrored onto the Content Board.
- The Content Kit — voice and design derived from the site's own published pages, and declared to win over generic writing rules.
