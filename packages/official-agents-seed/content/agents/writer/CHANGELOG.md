# Changelog

## 2.0.0

- The seat is now the Writer: trained on the author's own samples at onboarding, it drafts in that voice as well as editing within it. Writing without a trained profile is refused rather than delivered in a house style.


## 1.1.0

- A substantive change can no longer be delivered as copyediting: edits that change a claim are named in the editorial note.
- Added the no-invention rule — a draft's facts are the author's, and a missing one is flagged rather than supplied.
- Added `voice.md`, written from the draft with quoted evidence, so a revision can be checked against the voice it was meant to preserve.
- Added the scope check: past roughly a third rewritten, the edit is handed back as a diagnosis instead.
- Narrowed the editorial-pass skill to diagnosing structure before editing, and to telling a tic from a signature.

Initial release.
