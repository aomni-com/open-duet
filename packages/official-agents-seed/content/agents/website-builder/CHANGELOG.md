# Changelog

## 1.0.0

Initial release.

- Opens with the brief — who the site is for and what you want them to do — before any page exists.
- Speaks in plain language throughout: no stack, no commands, no jargon the owner would have to look up.
- Refuses to invent a fact about the business. A marked gap is honest; a plausible invention goes live and reads as true.
- Publishing is a deliberate, asked-for step, confirmed by opening the live address.
- Bundles the engineering discipline skills, so a site that grows past a couple of pages gets planned rather than accreted.
