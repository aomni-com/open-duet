# Sales Emails

**Goal**: Start a conversation, not close a deal. The sale comes later.

## Required Context

Before drafting, ensure you have:

- Who you're reaching out to (role, company)
- What you're offering and why it's relevant to them
- Any research or observations about their situation
- **Purpose of the email** — If unclear (cold outreach vs. warm intro vs. re-engagement), ask the user to clarify before drafting

## Structure

### Cold Outreach

1. **Observation** — Something specific you noticed about them or their company
2. **Connection** — Link that observation to a challenge they likely face
3. **Credibility** — Brief proof you understand this space (one sentence)
4. **Question** — Invite dialogue, not commitment

```
[Observation about their situation]

[How that connects to a challenge you can help with]

[Brief credibility: how you've helped similar situations]

[Question inviting conversation, not asking for a meeting]
```

**Length**: 25-50 words ideal. Never exceed 75.

### Shorter Alternative

When you have less context or want minimal commitment:

1. **Observation** — What you noticed
2. **Question** — Simple, curiosity-driven

```
[Observation]

[Question about whether this resonates]
```

### Competitor Situations

When they're using a competing solution:

1. **Acknowledge** — Recognize their current choice
2. **Question** — Ask about satisfaction without badmouthing
3. **Gap** — Highlight what might be missing (frame as question)

## What to Avoid

- Feature lists or product descriptions
- "I'd love to hop on a quick call"
- "Just checking in" or "Just following up"
- Multiple asks in one email
- Aggressive meeting requests
- Claims about their problems you can't substantiate

## Call-to-Action Patterns

Replace aggressive asks with conversation starters:

| Instead of                | Try                                            |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| "Can we schedule a call?" | "Is this worth exploring?"                     |
| "Are you free Thursday?"  | "Does this match what you're seeing?"          |
| "Let me show you a demo"  | "Would it help to see how others handle this?" |

## Personalization Requirements

Every sales email must include at least one:

- Specific observation about their company, role, or recent activity
- Connection to their industry or situation
- Reference that proves you researched them

Generic emails that could be sent to anyone will fail.
